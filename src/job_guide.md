Here's the guide I've been working on

Online Resources & Tools

  - **Built In:** This was one of the best sites I used. Check [builtin.com](http://builtin.com) for remote roles. I recommend using recency filters (e.g., past 3 days). I found LinkedIn to be less effective for job postings, though I still checked it weekly.
  - **Organization:** Consider setting up a dedicated Google Voice number and email address for applications. Using a business-tier email (like Google Workspace) can help with automation, but ensure your domain is configured correctly to avoid spam folders.
  - **Online Presence:** Aim to post or comment on LinkedIn 3–5 times per week to help the algorithm push your profile to recruiters. A personal portfolio site with strong SEO and an active or [active looking](https://github.com/artiebits/fake-git-history) GitHub is also beneficial.

The Interview Process

  - **Technical Prep:** Prepare for extensive coding tests, LeetCode, and take-home assignments. Interviews can range from basic "College 101" questions to highly specific tasks. Note that some companies encourage AI use while others strictly forbid it.
  - **Timeline:** Expect the total interview time per company to range from 3.5 to 14 hours.
  - **AI Screening:** Be prepared for some initial screenings conducted by AI video or chat agents.
  - **Rejection & Feedback:** The market is very competitive, and generic rejections are common even after strong interviews. Most companies have policies against providing specific feedback, though it doesn't hurt to ask.

Resume & Application Strategy

  - **Referrals:** These are far more effective than cold applications. Every referral I received resulted in at least two interview rounds. Reach out to your LinkedIn network; even loose connections are often willing to help.
  - **ATS Optimization:** Applicant Tracking Systems are rigorous. I moved my skills section to the top of my resume to ensure it's captured correctly.
  - **Reviewers:** I used [Resume Worded](https://resumeworded.com/score) for high-level guidance; they offer a few free reviews per month.
  - **Gaps:** Fill any recent employment gaps with open-source work or job-related activities to maintain momentum.
  - **Persistence:** Follow up repeatedly. Recruiter workflows can be disorganized, and follow-ups often prevent being ghosted.

Mindset & Market Reality

  - **Commitment:** Treat the search like a full-time job. Use non-interview time for upskilling, classes, or design challenges.
  - **Market Trends:** Be cautious of fake postings or roles with deflated salaries. I recommend standing firm on your requirements unless a change is absolutely necessary.
